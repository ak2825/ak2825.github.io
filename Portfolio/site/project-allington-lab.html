Track 1

Context

The Allington Lab studies agricultural land use in Uttarakhand, where satellite imagery can help distinguish active agricultural parcels as part of a broader classification workflow.

Track 2

Approach

My work connects visual interpretation in QGIS with the carefully prepared data needed for downstream modeling.

Track 3

Findings

I supported the creation of labeled parcel data and PyTorch preprocessing for the research pipeline, helping organize satellite imagery into model-ready inputs.

That preparation supports LSTM and Conv-LSTM approaches to land-use classification, where spatial information and change across imagery both matter.

Track 4

What I learned

I learned how much classification quality depends on the work before modeling: consistent parcel boundaries, careful labels, and preprocessing decisions all shape what a model can learn.

The project also showed me how geospatial tools and deep-learning workflows complement one another when the research question depends on both place and time.
